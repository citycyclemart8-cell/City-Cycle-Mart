About City Cycle Mart

Passionate about pedaling since 2020

Who We Are

City Cycle Mart was founded with a simple mission: to get more people on bikes. We believe that cycling is not just a mode of transport, but a way of life that promotes health, happiness, and a cleaner environment.

We source the finest bicycles from around the world, catering to every rider—whether you're a city commuter, a mountain trail blazer, or a weekend road warrior. Our team of expert mechanics ensures that every bike leaving our shop is tuned to perfection.

We are a customer-focused cycle shop with years of experience in understanding riders’ needs. Our goal is not just to sell cycles, but to help our customers choose the right cycle at the right price. We guide you honestly, based on your age, height, usage, and budget.

Join us in our journey to make the world a greener, fitter place, one pedal at a time.
